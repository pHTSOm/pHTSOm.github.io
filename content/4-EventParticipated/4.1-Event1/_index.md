# Summary Report: “FCAJ Community Day – 23/05”

### Event Objectives

- Share practical knowledge across AI, cloud, and real-world engineering through a series of community talks
- Show how context and modern AI tooling make AI genuinely useful in day-to-day work
- Explore AWS building blocks — from Amazon CloudFront to multi-agent systems — through speaker experience
- Learn from a student hackathon journey and connect the community of builders, professionals, and students

### Speakers & Sessions

- **Tinh Truong** – Platform Engineer, GotymeX — *Context Is Everything: Making AI Actually Work for You*
- **Anh Pham** – Cloud Consultant, G-AsiaPacific Vietnam — *Friendly AI Assistant with Amazon Quick*
- **Thinh Nguyen** – DevOps Engineer, FCAJ — *From Edge To Origin: CloudFront as Your Foundation*
- **Team VIB** (Thao Nguyen, Mai Nguyen, Uyen Le) — *36 hrs with LotusHacks – Building UTMorpho from Idea to Reality*
- **Duc Dao** – Solution Architect, Cloud Kinetics — *Non-Determinism of “Deterministic” LLM Settings*
- **Vy Lam** – Senior Business System Analyst — *Enterprise-Grade Multi-Agent System: The Case of Startup Credit Scoring*


### Key Highlights

#### Context Is Everything: Making AI Actually Work for You — Tinh Truong
- Why AI fails without context, and what “context” really means in practice.
- The shift from one-off prompts to memory — the idea of a **“Second AI Brain”** that carries context over time.
- How better context leads to better results: a practical mindset and everyday tips.
- Career insights and how students can start building with AI, followed by a Q&A.

#### Friendly AI Assistant with Amazon Quick — Anh Pham
- **Quick Chat Agent**: AI assistants for exploring data and analyzing insights.
- **Quick Flows**: building intelligent workflows with natural language — no coding required.
- **Quick Spaces**: shared collaborative spaces that turn individual insights into team knowledge.
- **Quick Sight**: building dashboards and reports from raw data using natural language.

#### From Edge To Origin: CloudFront as Your Foundation — Thinh Nguyen
- Amazon CloudFront as a foundation for every workload, not just static content delivery.
- **Cost optimization** with CloudFront.
- **Security capabilities** at the edge.
- **Enhanced reliability** with CloudFront.
- **Enhanced performance** for end users closer to the edge.

#### 36 hrs with LotusHacks – Building UTMorpho from Idea to Reality — Team VIB
- Why the team joined **LotusHacks** and what motivated them.
- From zero to idea — the brainstorming journey.
- Defining the problem and shaping the **UTMorpho** product.
- Building under pressure — a 36-hour development sprint.
- Challenges, failures, and the turning points along the way.
- UTMorpho product overview and live demo.
- Key learnings and what’s next for the project.

#### Non-Determinism of “Deterministic” LLM Settings — Duc Dao
- How LLMs choose the next token.
- The common assumption that **Temperature = 0** guarantees deterministic output.
- The reality: inference-time optimizations mean the same input can still produce different outputs.
- Practical impacts of this non-determinism on real applications.
- Mitigation strategies to make results more reproducible.

#### Enterprise-Grade Multi-Agent System: The Case of Startup Credit Scoring — Vy Lam
- The structural mismatch between traditional banking systems and startup data.
- Single-agent design: when to use it and when not to.
- The multi-agent paradigm as an alternative.
- A blueprint for a **“virtual credit committee.”**
- Guardrails and compliance in a regulated domain.
- Operational ROI and an implementation roadmap.


### Key Takeaways

- **Context is the real differentiator for AI.** Better memory and context — not just cleverer prompts — is what turns AI from a novelty into a dependable everyday tool.
- **Natural-language tooling lowers the barrier.** Tools like Amazon Quick let people build workflows, dashboards, and shared knowledge without heavy coding.
- **CloudFront is a foundation, not an afterthought.** Used well, it improves cost, security, reliability, and performance across many workloads.
- **Shipping under constraints teaches fast.** The LotusHacks/UTMorpho story showed how a clear problem and tight timeline force focus and rapid iteration.
- **“Deterministic” LLM settings aren’t fully deterministic.** Temperature = 0 is not a guarantee; production systems need mitigation strategies for reproducibility.
- **Multi-agent systems suit complex, regulated problems.** Splitting responsibilities across agents — with guardrails and compliance — can fit domains a single agent cannot.

#### Community and inspiration
- The day reinforced how much a community of builders, professionals, and students can learn from sharing experience openly.
- Several sessions gave me new ideas to explore in my own projects, especially around adding better context to AI workflows.


> Overall, FCAJ Community Day gave me a broad, practical view of where AI and cloud are heading, and left me with concrete ideas to try in my own work.
